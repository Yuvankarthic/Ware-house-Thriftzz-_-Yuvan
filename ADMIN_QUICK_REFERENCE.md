## 📋 5-STEP ORDER WORKFLOW

### 1️⃣ CLAIM ORDER
- View "New Order" status
- Click order row
- Click "🙋 Claim This Order"

### 2️⃣ COPY DETAILS
- Order panel opens
- Click "📋 Copy Details"
- Details ready to paste

### 3️⃣ BOOK DELIVERY
- Open Porter app
- Create shipment
- Paste copied details
- Pay & get tracking ref

### 4️⃣ UPDATE TRACKING
- Back in admin panel
- Enter Rider Phone
- Enter Tracking Reference
- Click "Save Delivery Info"

### 5️⃣ UPDATE STATUS
- Click "Move to: Out for Delivery"
- When arrived: "Move to: Delivered"
- Done! ✅
